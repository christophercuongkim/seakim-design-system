Decision · accent strategy

One brand colour everywhere, or one hue per app

Rejected

Single brand colour

  • One palette to maintain
  • Instant cross-product recognition
  • But: Bench and Voyage stop being distinguishable at a glance
  • But: the colour ends up owned by whichever app shipped first
Chosen

One hue per app

  • Achromatic shared layer, so nothing to renegotiate
  • Same lightness and chroma — contrast holds across apps
  • App three picks a hue and inherits everything else
  • Cost: no single colour means "SeaKim"
