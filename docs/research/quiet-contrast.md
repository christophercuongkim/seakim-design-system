# Quiet contrast: alpha hairlines, ink primary, app hues

Generated by `node tool/quiet-contrast.mjs` from `tokens/colors.css`, `tokens/theme-light.css`, `tokens/apps.css` (all generated from `tokens/src/color.tokens.json`). Method: `tool/oklch.mjs` — oklch to sRGB with chroma-reduction gamut mapping, WCAG 2.1 relative luminance, ratio `(L1+0.05)/(L2+0.05)`. Alpha hairlines are composited over the surface in sRGB (source-over) and then measured against that surface. Thresholds: 3:1 non-text (WCAG 1.4.11), 4.5:1 text (WCAG 1.4.3). `--surface-scrim` is a `color-mix()` and is not a backdrop, so it is omitted.

5. The first alpha that clears 3:1 on the worst surface is 48% ink in light (`--surface-shimmer`) and 41% white in dark (`--surface-active`), about four times the candidate range.
